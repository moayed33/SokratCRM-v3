# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

SokratCRM manages customer and donor relationships, including leads, follow-ups, campaigns, quotations, calendars, and operational reporting.

## Operating Context

Authenticated CRM staff work from a bilingual Arabic and English interface. Access is controlled by permissions, lead scope, and the currently selected branch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Campaigns have scheduled start and end dates and currently attached leads.
- Campaign conversion reporting uses the current share of attached leads whose status is `donor`.
- An ended campaign is one whose scheduled end date has passed.
- Historical lead status changes are not attributed to campaigns, so campaign conversion is presented as a current snapshot rather than a historical claim.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the SokratCRM name, existing logo assets, bilingual content, and established CRM interface.

## Product Principles

- Present operational metrics with explicit, trustworthy definitions.
- Keep reporting filterable and easy to scan.
- Respect permission, lead, and branch boundaries in every view.
- Support desktop and mobile workflows in both text directions.
